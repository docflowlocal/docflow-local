# Community Code of Conduct

DocFlow Local is committed to a respectful, technically constructive community.

Be welcoming, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, protect confidential information, and give actionable feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports may be sent privately to `conduct@docflowlocal.com`.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
